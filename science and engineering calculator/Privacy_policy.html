Privacy Policy

Science & Engineering Calculator (package com.drylu_ian.science_engineering_calculator)
Effective date: November 10, 2026

Summary. The app is a local calculator. Formulas, parameters, history, tables and settings stay on your device. A local zip backup can be created. The only third-party component is Google AdMob for banner ads.

1. Local data

Formulas, equations, calculation chains, categories, table contents, parameters, history and settings are stored in the app's private storage (files/scicalc-data.json). We have no server, require no account and do not upload or read your calculation content.

2. Backup and restore

You can create a zip backup from the side menu. The file is written to:

Android/data/com.drylu_ian.science_engineering_calculator/files/backups

The app does not request storage permission, does not upload backups and does not read files from other apps. Backups are deleted with the app when it is uninstalled.

3. Advertising

The app uses the Google AdMob SDK to display adaptive banner ads at the bottom of the navigation drawer, home tabs except Favorites, calculation pages and the history page. AdMob may collect the advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, network state, and ad impression/click data for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. It is not used by us to identify your calculations. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, consent is requested through Google UMP, including in the EEA. If personalised ads are refused, they are not loaded. You can change or withdraw consent in the in-app ad privacy settings or reset/delete your advertising ID in system settings.

4. Permissions

The app requests only INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for the ad SDK. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage permissions.

5. Sharing, retention and contact

We do not sell your data. Local data remains until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Contact: drylu123@gmail.com.
