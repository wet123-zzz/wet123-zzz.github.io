Privacy Policy

Science & Engineering Calculator (package com.lww.scicalc)
Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Quick summary. The app is a local calculator. Formulas, parameters, history, tables and settings are stored on your device and are not uploaded. The only third-party component is Google AdMob, which serves banner ads. A zip backup can be written to the app's own private folder and is never uploaded.

1. Who we are

This app is developed and published by drylu123 (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions, contact drylu123@gmail.com.

2. Data you create

The app stores the following items locally on your device:

  • calculation formulas, equations, calculation chains and categories;
  • parameters, units, ranges, notes and rounding settings;
  • table database contents and table references;
  • calculation history snapshots;
  • app settings such as theme, language and reduced-motion preference.

These items are stored in the app's private storage (files/scicalc-data.json). We do not have a server, do not require an account, and do not upload or read this content.

3. Backup and restore

You can create a local backup from the side menu. A backup is a zip file containing the app's data snapshot, written to the app-specific external folder:

Android/data/com.lww.scicalc/files/backups

The app does not request storage permission, does not upload backup files and does not read files from other apps. Backups are deleted together with the app when you uninstall it. Restoring a backup only reads zip files from the folder above.

4. Advertising

The app displays banner ads through the Google AdMob SDK. Banner slots are adaptive to the available width and may appear at the bottom of the navigation drawer, home tabs (except the Favorites tab), calculation result pages, and the history page.

AdMob may collect and process:

  • advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location;
  • network state and connection information;
  • ad impressions, clicks and related diagnostics.

This information is used for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. It is not used by us to identify the content of your calculations. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent, including for users in the European Economic Area. If you decline personalised ads, personalised advertising is not loaded. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the ad privacy options in the app or by resetting/deleting your advertising ID in system settings.

5. Permissions

The app requests only INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, which are used by the ad SDK. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage permissions.

6. Sharing and sale

We do not sell your data. The only third-party data recipient is Google's AdMob SDK as described above. We do not share your formulas, history or table contents with anyone.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's private files and its backups folder. Deleting data cannot be undone; please keep your own backup if you need it.

8. Children

The app is a general-purpose engineering tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Where required, the app will ask you to review and accept an updated in-app agreement before continuing to use the service.

10. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this policy: drylu123@gmail.com.

这些信息用于广告投放、频次控制、效果测量、统计分析与防欺诈。我们不会用 它们识别你的计算内容。Google 对数据的处理方式见 Google 隐私权政策。
