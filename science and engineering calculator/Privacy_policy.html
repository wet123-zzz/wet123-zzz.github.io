Privacy Policy (English)

Scientific & Engineering Calculator · Package com.drylu_ian.FormulaKit · Effective 2026-11-10

  1. This app is a local scientific calculator. Formulas, parameters, calculation history, tables and settings are stored only on this device, in the app's private internal storage (scicalc-data.json). No account, sign-in, cloud sync, clipboard access or file import/export of your formulas is provided.
  2. To show ads, the app integrates the Google AdMob SDK. The SDK may collect the advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, network state, and ad impression and click data. This information is used for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention, and is not used to identify the content of your calculations.
  3. Where consent is required (EEA, UK, Switzerland), Google's UMP consent form appears on first launch. You can review or withdraw consent at any time via Ad privacy settings in the app's side menu. If consent is refused, personalised ads are not loaded; non-personalised ads may still be requested where permitted by law.
  4. The app does not upload your formulas, parameters, history, tables or settings.
  5. Permissions: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, used only for ads. The app requests no storage permission on any Android version.
  6. Storage location and backup: on Android 10 and later, a backup zip is written to the system Download/SciCalc folder through MediaStore.Downloads, which needs no storage permission. On Android 9 and earlier the file goes to the app-specific external folder Android/data/com.drylu_ian.FormulaKit/files/backups. Backup files are never uploaded. On Android 10 and later they remain after the app is uninstalled and can be reopened after reinstalling via Choose file in Backup & restore, using the system file picker (only the file you pick is read). On Android 9 and earlier backups are removed together with the app. You can delete backup files at any time in the system file manager.
  7. Deletion: uninstalling the app deletes all app data stored in private storage. Backup zips are ordinary files under your control and are not deleted by the app unless you remove them.
  8. Contact: drylu123@gmail.com. Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
